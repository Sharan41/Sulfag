# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - For Your Client

**Simple 2-page guide for managing products**

---

## 🔐 Login

**Go to:** https://app.contentful.com  
**Login** with your Contentful account

**Direct link to products:** https://app.contentful.com/spaces/kvec8c4ex2a6/entries

---

## ✏️ Edit a Product

1. Click on any product
2. Change any field
3. Click **"Publish"** (top right)
4. ✅ Done! Changes appear on website instantly

---

## ➕ Add a New Product

1. Click **"Add entry"** → Select **"Product"**
2. Fill in all fields:
   - **Product Name:** e.g., "ACEPHATE 75% SP"
   - **Brand:** e.g., "SULFAG"
   - **Packing:** e.g., "10X1 KG; 20X500 GM"
   - **Crops:** e.g., "Cotton, Rice, Sugarcane"
   - **Target Pests:** e.g., "Jassid, Bollworm"
   - **Category:** Type exactly: `insecticides`, `fungicides`, `herbicides`, or `specialty`
   - **ID:** Enter a unique number (e.g., 50)
3. Click **"Publish"**
4. ✅ Product appears on website instantly!

---

## 🗑️ Delete a Product

1. Click on the product
2. Click **"..."** menu → **"Delete"**
3. Confirm
4. ✅ Product removed instantly!

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- **Always click "Publish"** - not just "Save"
- **Category** must be exact: `insecticides`, `fungicides`, `herbicides`, or `specialty`
- **ID** must be unique (don't duplicate)
- Changes appear on website in 10-30 seconds
