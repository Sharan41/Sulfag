# 📖 Sulfag Products Website - Client Guide

**Welcome!** This guide will help you manage your products on the website.

---

## 🎯 What You Can Do

You have **full control** over your product listings. You can:
- ✅ Edit existing products
- ✅ Add new products
- ✅ Delete products
- ✅ All changes appear on your website instantly!

**No coding knowledge required!** Everything is done through a simple web interface.

---

## 🔐 Getting Started

### Step 1: Login

1. Go to: **https://app.contentful.com**
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Login"

### Step 2: Access Your Products

Once logged in, you'll see your dashboard. Click on:
- **"Content"** (left sidebar)
- Or go directly to: **https://app.contentful.com/spaces/kvec8c4ex2a6/entries**

You'll see a list of all your products!

---

## 📝 Common Tasks

### Editing a Product

1. **Click** on any product from the list
2. **Change** any information you want
3. **Click "Publish"** (green button, top right)
4. **Done!** Your changes are live on the website

### Adding a New Product

1. **Click "Add entry"** (top right)
2. **Select "Product"**
3. **Fill in the form:**
   - Product Name: Full name of the product
   - Brand: Your brand name
   - Packing: Package sizes (e.g., "10X1 KG; 20X500 GM")
   - Crops: Which crops it's for (e.g., "Cotton, Rice")
   - Target Pests: What it targets (e.g., "Jassid, Bollworm")
   - Category: Choose one: `insecticides`, `fungicides`, `herbicides`, or `specialty`
   - ID: A unique number (don't use one that already exists)
4. **Click "Publish"**
5. **Done!** Your new product is on the website

### Deleting a Product

1. **Click** on the product
2. **Click "..."** (three dots menu, top right)
3. **Click "Delete"**
4. **Confirm** the deletion
5. **Done!** Product removed from website

---

## ⚠️ Important Tips

1. **Always click "Publish"** - Don't just save as draft
2. **Category must be exact:** Use exactly: `insecticides`, `fungicides`, `herbicides`, or `specialty`
3. **Use unique ID numbers** - Don't duplicate
4. **Changes appear in 10-30 seconds** - Be patient!
