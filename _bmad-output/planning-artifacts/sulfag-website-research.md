### About the Industry

> It plays a vital role in modern agriculture by protecting crops from pests, weeds, and plant diseases, ultimately helping farmers improve quality and quantity. By safeguarding plants at every stage of growth, it ensures that farmers can meet the increasing food demands of a growing population, making it an essential pillar of global food security.
>
> The industry is also one of the most closely regulated sectors, with strict standards for safety, efficacy, and environmental impact. Over the years, it has evolved significantly as both government bodies and consumers have become more conscious of health and sustainability.
>
> The agro-chemical formulations industry manufactures pesticide products such as insecticides, herbicides, weedicides and fungicides by converting technical-grade active ingredients into usable forms like liquids, powders, and granules. It plays a vital role in modern agriculture by protecting crops from pests, weeds, and diseases, helping increase yields and ensure food security.
>
> This industry supports global food production by helping reduce crop losses, which can otherwise reach 30–40% due to pests. It operates under strict regulations because pesticides must be effective while minimizing risk to humans and the environment.
>
> The market is driven by increasing food demand, growing agricultural intensification, and the need for reliable crop protection solutions.
>
> Major players include multinational crop-science companies, regional manufacturers that supply domestic and export markets.

├── Contact form
├── Location map

**Key Differentiators to Highlight:**

**Design Approach:**
- Use TT Chocolates for headings
