#### 📸 PARALLAX IMAGE REQUIREMENT (Important!)

For the premium "scroll effect" (like Anglo Gulf's Services section), we need a **tall agricultural image**:

| Specification | Requirement |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Minimum size** | 1920 x 1500 pixels (width x height) |
| **Ideal size** | 1920 x 1700 or 2560 x 1700 pixels |
| **Content** | Farmland, crop rows, agricultural field |
| **Format** | JPG (high quality) |
| **File size** | 200-500KB (will be optimized) |

**Why so tall?** The image stays fixed while the page scrolls over it. If the image is too short, the effect won't work properly!

**Good examples:**
- Rows of green crops stretching to horizon
- Rice paddy or wheat field panorama
- Sugarcane/cotton field aerial view
- Indian farmland at sunrise/sunset

**Questions for client:**
- [ ] Do you have professional photos of your facility/factory?
- [ ] Do you have photos of your product packaging?
- [ ] Do you have a TALL (1920x1500+) agricultural field photo for the parallax section?
- [ ] Can we use stock images for agricultural scenes, or do you have real photos?
- [ ] Do you want team/founder photos on the website?
